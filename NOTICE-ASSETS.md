## Commonwear 3026 additions

The future wrap, trousers, flares, cocoon pants, culottes, screen leggings, shorts, vest, bolero, sash, mantle, screen top, cuffs, belt and gaiters are original procedural cuts and shaped offsets of the MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 tights helper. The orbital halo geometry and animated screen patterns are generated by this repository's MIT-licensed code. The collection introduces no additional third-party asset sources. Its outfit presets reuse the existing sneakers, whose source notice remains above.

## Children

- **`youth` in the character config** (0 grown up .. 1 a child of about five, 0.5 about ten): child proportions laid over the adult body. The body is smaller, the head bigger (beyond the races' cap), the eyes bigger, the face softer (smaller nose and jaw, softer brow), and the shoulders, chest and hips narrower, with shorter limbs and a slimmer neck. The face is the youngest adult face; no child morph targets are shipped.
- **A child never shows anatomy.** `Character` turns `anatomy` off whenever `youth` > 0 and gives a child a flat chest and a slight build.
- `RaceSettings.youth` and `YOUTH` in `@actors/human` carry it through race shaping (bone scales, head scale, overall scale, face and body features), so a dwarf or halfling child works the same way. The viewer has a Youth slider under Race.

## Clothes over clothes

- **Fitted garments sit on the fitted garments worn under them.** A skirt over a chemise, or a tunic over hose, used to be fitted to the body each on its own, so the inner one cut through the outer wherever it lay further out. Now the outer one is lifted 3 mm over the inner one's surface there, in drawing order (layer, then outfit order). Before, only the garments cut from the body were lifted over. The lift is redone when the outfit or its order changes, but not when only the detail level changes.
- **Seated laps:** the leg colliders push skirt-skinned cloth straight off a level thigh, where they used to push it sideways, along the thigh. A tunic's skirt no longer sinks into the lap, so the hose and skin under it stop flickering through (WebGL and WebGPU).

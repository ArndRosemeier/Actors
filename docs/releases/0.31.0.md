## Backlit ears, darker creases, wet skin

Three more steps toward real skin, all in both skin shaders (WebGL and WebGPU):

- **Backlit glow**: thin skin (ears, nose wings, hands) glows red where you look toward a light through it, as real ears do against the sun. It is part of skin detail (`skinDetail`).
- **Darker creases**: eyelids, nose wings and the folds of the ear are a little darker. The shader measures the surface's curvature per pixel (no extra textures or passes) and darkens where it folds inward. Also part of skin detail.
- **Wet skin**: a new `skinWetness` setting (0 to 1, "Wet skin" under Look, 0 by default) makes skin glossy in uneven patches and a little darker: sweat, rain or a swim. `ThreeActor.skin.uWet` drives it directly, so a game can let a character sweat as they run.

Both detail additions cost a handful of instructions per skin pixel; wet skin costs nothing at 0. `apps/viewer/scripts/skin.mjs` now also shoots a backlit view, and `WET=0.8` renders wet skin.

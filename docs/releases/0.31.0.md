## One draw per outfit at the medium level, lighter small animals

Follows 0.29.0's crowd work, for people a few metres away and for animals.

- **The whole outfit in one mesh at the medium level.** From `actor.mergeOutfitFrom` (default 1, the medium level) every fitted garment is drawn as one skinned mesh. Their textures are packed into one shared atlas (people wearing the same outfit share it), and each garment keeps its tint, hem cut, roughness and metalness per vertex. In the viewer's medium-level smoke shot a dressed person went from 16 draws to 10; level 0 is unchanged. Skirts and other garments that use the leg colliders stay separate. `actor.mergeOutfitFrom = Infinity` turns it off. Works on WebGL and WebGPU. While merged, a garment's own mesh in `actor.meshes` is hidden: ask `actor.isVisible(name)` whether it is worn.
- **Small animals are lighter away from the camera.** Each animal now has a triangle budget for its medium and crowd levels: squirrel 5,000 / 1,200 (and it switches at 2 m and 6 m), cat and chicken 6,000 / 1,500, dog 8,000 / 2,000, horse and cow 12,000 / 3,000. The full level is unchanged.

Re-run `npm run import:animals` for the new animal budgets (or install the new release packs). The outfit merge works with existing human packs.

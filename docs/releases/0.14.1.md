## Procedural skin tones as seen

The procedural skin (`skin: "procedural"`) was almost white at the light end of `skinTone`: its tones were sRGB values read as linear, so a tone of 0.2 came out about #edd3c1. They are now read as sRGB, so `skinTone` runs from a light flesh tone (#edc2a8) through tan and brown to dark brown, as intended. Photo skin and `skinColor` are unchanged. `skinToneColor(tone)` gives the colour for a tone.

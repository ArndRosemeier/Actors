## Loose clothes that move

Skirts, dresses, robes and tunics hung from the body like statues: running, stopping or spinning left the hem exactly where the skinning put it. A new, optional `clothMotion` setting ("Loose clothes swing" in the outfit panel, on by default) makes them move:

- **Moving** leaves the hem trailing slightly behind.
- **Stopping** swings it forward and back until it settles.
- **Turning** quickly makes the skirt lag the hips and catch up.

It is not a cloth simulation. One point per character follows the hips on a damped spring (on the CPU, a few dozen operations a frame), and the vertex shader swings the cloth below the hips by that much, more the lower it hangs. Cloth against a leg stays with the leg, cloth swung into a leg stops at it, and the existing leg colliders still push it out. Both renderers have it (WebGL and WebGPU). Swings are kept small so the cloth never tears away from the legs. It needs the character to move in the world: clips that walk on the spot only sway a little.

`ThreeActor.clothMotion` switches it directly, and `ThreeActor.clothSway` exposes the spring. `apps/viewer/scripts/sway.mjs` shoots running, stopping and spinning frames with it on and off.

## Marines' cross belts stay whole and grey hair has no brown speckle in a crowd

Two leftovers of the torn-coats fix, both at the crowd level.

- **Cross belts.** The reduced belt strip (overlapping straps) bent, widened and opened slivers, and the coarse coat under it sagged by more than the belt's gap. The cross belts are no longer reduced at all (a few hundred triangles), and the coats, lapels and tailcoats keep half their triangles at the crowd level (`COAT_LODS` in tools/import-makehuman/src/navy.ts).
- **Hair speckle.** The reduced hair cards leave holes through which the scalp's skin showed as brown specks in grey hair, seen from behind. Body vertices with hair just above them (`scalpVertices`, packages/three/src/scalp.ts) are now drawn in the hair's colour at the crowd level and in the crowd figures. Face, ears and neck stay skin.

Needs a regenerated pack (`npm run import:human`); the release's packs have it. No API changes.

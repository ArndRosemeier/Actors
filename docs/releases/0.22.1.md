## Library animals: no shrinking, floating feet fixed

- **The loader ignores scale and turn tracks on non-joint nodes.** A clip may key a plain node (the elk's `RigRoot`, scale 0.01 in its Walk clip) that is not a bone; that shrank the whole animal to 1.3% while it played, and turned it in Run and Sprint. `ignoreBodyTracks(model, clip)` drops those tracks when a clip is read (positions of such nodes stay, they carry root motion and are taken out by `extractTravel`).
- **The foot pass works when a clip lifts the whole body.** Whether a foot is planted is now judged against the lowest foot of the same pose, not against the rest pose, so an animation that holds the body above its rest place still leans, lowers and reaches. Body parts that hang low but are not feet (a turtle's head and tail) lift the body but are not reached to the ground.
- **The pass refuses a ground that is more than half a body height off** (a wrong frame, a teleport) and leaves the animal as animated, instead of dragging it there.

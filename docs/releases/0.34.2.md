## Only humans: no leaf hair or crest fins

- **Race hairstyles follow `allow.races`.** The generated hairstyles "Leaves (sylvan)" and "Crest fins (drakeborn)" belong to their races (`PROCEDURAL_HAIR_RACE`). A game that leaves those races out (`allow: { races: ["human"] }`) no longer offers them in the Hair picker, and `set` takes them off a loaded look.

## Library animals: feet on the ground, whatever the size

`libraryAnimal.setGround((x, z) => height | null)` runs a foot-to-ground pass after the pose of every `update` (after the clip and after the deform's scales), so it holds for any size, deform, clip and rig the loader reads:

- the soles are found in the skin (the lowest points of the deformed rest pose, on bones near a leaf, grouped into feet by the skeleton), not from joints, so a thick giant foot or a flat sole does not sink;
- the body leans to the plane through the planted feet, is lifted so no sole is in the ground (at once; it settles down at most four body heights a second), and each leg's bones (a chain of three from the foot up, CCD over all the foot's points, each bone within 40 degrees of the animation) reach so the sole meets the ground;
- a foot the animation has lifted in a step is not pulled down; an animal in a jump or a fall (a foot more than 30% of its height above the ground) is left alone.

`FootingOptions` sets `maxTilt`, `reach`, `chain` and `bend`. `libraryAnimal.feet?.readings` has the last pass's soles and ground for tests and checks. Pass null to take it off (for the dead, flyers, swimmers). With a ground set, place `object` near the ground; the pass sets the model's height, lean and leg bones itself.

`setDeform` now measures the lowest point of the skin (not the leaf joints) when it lowers a deformed animal, so thick feet no longer sink into the ground at rest.

`syntheticWalker(legs, legLength?, clips?)` and `soleClearances(animal, mesh, ground)` (a skinned test animal with flat soles, and how high each foot's skin stands over a ground) let a host check its own ground function with no model files.

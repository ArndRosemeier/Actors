## Themes: a game offers only what fits its setting

The character generator's options carry theme tags, and a game passes the themes it allows:

- Garments, hairstyles and races have `themes` in the catalog (`GARMENT_THEMES`, `HAIR_THEMES`, `RACE_THEMES`). Themes: `modern`, `vintage`, `future`, `antique`, `medieval`, `nordic`, `eastern`, `fantasy`; untagged options (underwear, plain socks, most hairstyles, humans) fit every setting.
- `Character.load({ themes: { themes: ["medieval", "fantasy"], without: ["real-faith"] } })` (and `createCharacterGenerator`): the character, its editor and `randomize` then offer only what fits. Outfit presets stay when all their garments do; robots come with `future`, skeletons with `fantasy`.
- Flags for things to leave out whatever the themes: `real-faith` marks real-world religious symbols (the templar knight helmet, the Santa hat).
- `filterCatalog(catalog, filter)` and `fitsThemes(tags, filter)` for catalogs you hold.
- `randomize({ race: "any" })` picks one of the catalog's races.
- Viewer: `?themes=medieval,fantasy&without=real-faith`.

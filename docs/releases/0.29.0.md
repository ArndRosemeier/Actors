## Cheaper crowds: one draw per far person, fewer shadows, slower far animation

Measured in MasterWorld, a town of 30 people cost about 2 ms of CPU a frame, and most of it was per-object work in three.js: draws, shadow draws and bone matrices. This release cuts those, for every game on Actors, with no change to how a character looks up close.

- **Hair joins the crowd mesh.** At the crowd level, scalp hair is merged into the one mesh with the body and clothes, cut by its own texture's alpha and kept two-sided. A far person is now one draw and one shadow draw instead of two of each. Skirts (they need the leg colliders) and add-ons stay their own meshes. `actor.crowdHair = false` keeps the hair separate.
- **Small parts cast no shadow.** Eyes, teeth, tongue, lashes and brows no longer draw into the shadow map: their shadows fall inside the head or are too small to see. `actor.smallPartShadows = true` brings them back.
- **Frustum culling.** Every mesh of an actor is culled against one generous sphere around the body (`actor.bounds()`), so people behind the camera are no longer drawn (they still cast shadows into view). Off while clips play with root motion, or with `actor.frustumCulling = false`.
- **Slower animation far away and off screen.** At the crowd level the clip, grips and bone uploads advance every 2nd frame; off screen every 4th (`Character.update` checks the view against `lodCamera`). No time is lost, the pose just moves in bigger steps. Tune with `actor.animationSteps` (per level, `[]` = every frame) and `actor.offscreenStep`.
- **Fix: no double draws at the crowd level.** A garment shown and hidden again before the next frame made the merged body and clothes visible again beside the crowd mesh.
- **Lighter crowd and medium levels.** At the crowd level every garment is now at most 1,500 triangles and hair at most 1,200 (some gowns, boots and helmets kept 40,000 to 72,000: their meshes are loose triangles the simplifier could not reduce, so the crowd level now welds them first; it draws garments in flat colours, so welding costs nothing visible). At the medium level garments are capped at 12,000 and hair at 6,000.

Re-run `npm run import:human` (or install the new release pack) for the lighter levels; the rest works with older packs.

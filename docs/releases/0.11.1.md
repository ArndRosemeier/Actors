## Shared wardrobes and reversed depth

Two fixes for apps that draw many characters with three's WebGPURenderer:

- A character sharing a pack (`shareWith`) now shows a garment or hairstyle that another character loaded first. Before, it showed nothing, so apps had to load the whole wardrobe into the shared pack before making any character.
- With a reversed depth buffer (`renderer.reversedDepthBuffer`), garment layers are drawn over the skin again. Their polygon offsets are flipped when the character is made with that renderer (`Character.load({ renderer })`, or `new ThreeActor(runtime, kit, { reversedDepth: true })`); `reverseOffsets(material)` does it for materials of your own.

## Shoes that fit

- **Shoes and boots sit closer to the foot.** MakeHuman's shoes are modelled loose: the sneakers stood about 14 mm off each side of the foot, so they looked a size or two too big. The importer now pulls every shoe towards the foot, keeping a third of each gap beyond 4 mm (`SHOE_SNUG`, or `snug` per wardrobe item). Soles keep their thickness, a sole rim that stands out sideways comes in, and the opening keeps its room so the ankle can still move. Regenerate the packs (`npm run import:human`).
- **No more leg cut away above cuffed boots.** The skin under a shoe is hidden so it can't poke through, but the leg just inside a flared boot top (cuffed, biker, viking, riding and medieval boots, pointed shoes) was hidden too, so you saw a jagged hole above the boot. Under shoes, boots and socks the importer now casts a few rays from each skin vertex: skin that can be seen through the opening stays, skin fully inside is hidden even next to an edge. That also closes skin showing through the seams between a shoe's pieces (the work boots). Skin with nothing over it keeps the old rule (hidden unless near the opening), so toes that reach the top of a riding boot still don't poke through.
- New check: `apps/viewer/scripts/shoes.mjs` shoots the feet in every shoe (female and male, side, front and mid-stride) to `screenshots/shoes/`.

## What's new: a cat

`@actors/animal` now has a cat on the same rig and bones as the dog and squirrel, so the shared clips play on it too.

- **Maine Coon, modelled on a side-on photo.** The rig is fitted to the photo's joints, and the trunk, neck and skull follow its outline. It has almond eye openings, whisker pads, toes, ear tufts and a bushy tail. About 44k triangles.
- **Whiskers:** thin tubes on the whisker pads and above the eyes, rigid on the nose and head bones (a separate `whiskers` mesh).
- **Cat coat in the fur material:** mackerel tabby stripes (bars on the sides, rings on legs and tail, lines on the forehead, a dark spine line), tortoiseshell patches, light inner ears and a nose leather colour. The animal eye takes a slit pupil (`pupil`).
- **12 clips:** the dog's clips, with bark and howl renamed meow and yowl, fetch left out and more tail swing.
- **Five presets:** brown tortie tabby (the base), brown tabby, red tabby, silver tabby and black.
- Gaps: one body (only the bulk morph; the bone proportion sliders work), no cat-specific motion yet (grooming, tail held up), and the overall shape is still prototype quality.

Dogs, squirrels and deer are unchanged.

## How to use it

```ts
import { Animal } from "@actors/animal";

const cat = await Animal.load(`${packs}/cat/manifest.json`);
cat.setPreset("redTabby");          // see cat.presets (CAT_BREEDS)
```

- The cat uses the dog's shape and coat types (`DogShape`, `DogCoat`). New coat fields: `tabby`, `patches`, `innerEar`, `pupil`, `nose` (plain leather colour) and `strand` (strand spacing).
- `animalMaterials(uniforms)` draws the whiskers with their own material.
- The cat pack is in `actors-packs-0.6.3.tar.gz` (`npx actors-packs`) and on the hosted packs. The demo shows it at `animals.html?animal=cat`.

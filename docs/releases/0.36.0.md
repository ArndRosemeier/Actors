## Age-of-sail navies

- **A navy wardrobe for about 1800 with fourteen presets** in a new "Navy" group, seven for the Royal Navy and seven for the French Marine: captain, lieutenant (French: officer), midshipman (aspirant), boatswain, seaman, seaman at work, and marine. Made for the brig crews of Nauta.
- **Generated pieces** (`navy-*`): seaman's shirt, wide trousers (plain or striped), short jacket, waistcoat, knee breeches, gaiters, officer's tailcoat, marine's coatee, lapels, standing collar and cuffs (each its own garment, so each takes its navy's colour), neck stock, cross belts; buttons, epaulettes (pair or right shoulder), neckerchief, bicorne with gold lace and a black or tricolour cockade, round hat and a knitted cap.
- New theme `age-of-sail`; the buckled shoes fit it too.
- Cut pieces can carry their own texture coordinates and a print drawn at import (the stripes run down the leg).
- New check: `apps/viewer/scripts/navy.mjs` shoots every navy preset, including the view down from the eyes. See docs/navy-wardrobe.md. Regenerate the packs (`npm run import:human`).

## Crowd level fixes

- Garments drawn beside the merged crowd mesh (skirt-skinned coats, surcoats) no longer keep their layer's polygon offset there, which put them in front of the lapels, belts and buttons merged over them.

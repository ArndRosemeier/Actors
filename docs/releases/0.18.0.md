## Chickens

- **A new animal: the chicken** (`@actors/animal` species `chicken`, pack `chicken`). It is a brown laying hen modelled on a photo, about 0.42 m to the top of the comb, built on the skeleton of Mesh2Motion's CC0 bird rig.
- **Seven clips of its own:** idle, walk (with the head bob), run, peck, scratch, flap and sit. The wings are separate surfaces, so they open in `flap`.
- **Eight breed presets:** brown hen (the default), White Leghorn, Black Australorp, Barred Plymouth Rock, Light Sussex, Buff Orpington, Silver Laced Wyandotte and Speckled Sussex. Shape sliders cover size, legs, neck, head, tail, bulk, fluff, comb (rose to tall single comb) and wattles.
- **Drawn feathers in the fur material** (WebGL and WebGPU): new coat options `comb`, `legs`, `featherSize`, `shaft`, `lacing`, `barring`, `hackleStripe`, `tailDark`, `mottle`, `fluffLight` and `tailWhite`. Other animals are unchanged.
- **Importer:** body parts can sit on separate surface layers (`BodyPart.layer`), meshed and skinned on their own.

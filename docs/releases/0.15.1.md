## Clothes that stay their colour and layers that stay apart

Fixes found on MasterWorld's townsfolk:

- **Tinted garments no longer turn white.** A tint scales each pixel by its brightness over the texture's mean. On dark, high-contrast textures (a gored skirt, a monk's hood) the bright patches got up to three times the tint and came out white. The gain now has a soft knee above 1, so it stays under 2, and dark textures keep their pattern. Hair tints too.
- **Garments on the same layer no longer cut through each other.** A chemise and a skirt, both cut from the body, shared one polygon offset, so they speckled through each other where they overlap. `ThreeActor.setGarmentOrder(names)` takes the outfit inner first, and each later garment on the same layer is drawn a quarter layer further out. `Character` passes its outfit.
- **Colours picked by eye are sRGB.** `srgb(r, g, b)` makes one. The default skin tone, teeth and mouth now use it, and the colours that are meant to be linear say so.

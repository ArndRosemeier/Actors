## What's new: a deer

`@actors/animal` now has a deer next to the dogs and squirrels.

- **Sika hind, modelled on a photo.** The body is generated like the dog's (trunk, neck and skull from the photo's profile, muscled legs with cannons, dewclaws and cloven hooves, large cupped ears, almond eyes with preorbital glands, nose pad with nostrils). About 52k triangles.
- **13 clips from Quaternius' CC0 low-poly Deer** (its skeleton and animations are used, not its mesh): three idles, eating, walk, gallop, leap, jump, headbutt, kick, two hit reactions and death.
- **Six presets:** sika hind (the base), fallow doe, chital, red deer hind, roe doe and white-tailed doe.
- **Deer coat markings in the fur material:** sika spots in rows beside a dark dorsal stripe, a pale flank line, the white rump patch with its black border, a greyer head and neck, eye ring, pale inner ears and white tufts over the metatarsal glands.
- **Sliders:** legs, back, neck, head, ears, tail and size, plus bulk, belly, neck girth and a longer face.
- Gaps: no jaw bone (the mouth stays closed) and no antlers yet.

Dogs and squirrels are unchanged.

## How to use it

```ts
import { Animal, type DeerCoat, type DeerShape } from "@actors/animal";

const deer = await Animal.load<DeerShape, DeerCoat>(`${packs}/deer/manifest.json`);
deer.setPreset("fallow");          // see deer.presets
```

- `Animal` takes its species (dog, squirrel or deer) from the pack. The type parameters only type `shape` and `coat`. `setPreset` is new; `setBreed` still works.
- `setFurCoat(uniforms, deer.coat, deer.shape.size)` draws the deer markings. A deer coat has every field the fur material needs.
- The deer pack is in `actors-packs-0.6.2.tar.gz` (`npx actors-packs`) and on the hosted packs. The demo shows it at `animals.html?animal=deer`.

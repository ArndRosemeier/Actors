## Garments no longer tear under a reversed depth buffer

Found in Nauta (WebGPU, `depth: 'reversed'`): navy coats showed torn white patches at the shoulders, collars, chest and hems, because the shirt, waistcoat and trousers under them were drawn in front.

- A garment is drawn over the layers under it by a polygon offset. Under a reversed float depth buffer the slope-scaled part of that offset is not millimetres but centimetres on a tilted surface (a sleeve, a shoulder seen from the side), so a lower layer won there. Now a reversed buffer gets units only (`reversedOffset` in `@actors/three`), which grow with the distance in proportion to the depth's own precision, as a layer's few millimetres of room need. Forward depth buffers (WebGL, WebGPU without `reversedDepthBuffer`) are unchanged.
- Hair cards drawn blended keep their pull in the same way.

No pack changes: existing packs work unchanged.

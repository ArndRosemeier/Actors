## Cows graze standing square

- **The cow's `eat` clip no longer splays the forelegs.** The cow borrows its clips from Mesh2Motion's horse rig, and that horse's "Eating" drops the chest and spreads the forelegs in a wide V. On a cow this looked like broken legs. Now the cow's legs, back and tail keep the idle, and only the neck and head go down. The neck is also swung forward, so the muzzle comes down in front of the hooves, about 0.35 m above the ground. The importer has a new `borrow` clip option for this. The horse's own `eat` is unchanged.
- **The Braunvieh coat is darker.** The base colour is now grey-brown `#635343` (was `#84745f`), which no longer looks almost white in daylight.

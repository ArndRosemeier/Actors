## Fur on WebGPU

Animal fur and coats now draw with three's WebGPURenderer (node materials), not just the WebGL renderer:

- `animalNodeMaterials(uniforms)`: a material kit for animal packs with the fur body (coat patterns, sheen), animal eyes, tack and whiskers as node materials. Pass it to `ThreeActor`, then call `setFurCoat(uniforms, coat, size)` as before.
- `addNodeFurShells(actor, uniforms)`: the fur shells (one instanced draw) with a node material, the WebGPU twin of `addFurShells`. `addFurShellsWith(actor, makeMaterial)` takes any shell material.
- All animals of a kind share one shader; each animal's coat reaches it as per-object uniforms.
- A pack's extra vertex attributes (coat and fur flow) are packed into one interleaved buffer, and animals get no face morph channels, so a horse stays within WebGPU's 8 vertex buffers and 16 attributes.
- The skinned fur flow is available to node graphs as `skinnedFlow` (dual-quaternion skinning).

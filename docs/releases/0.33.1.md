## Eyes at the crowd level

Found in MasterWorld, where every townsperson but the few nearest is drawn at the crowd level, however close:

- **Eyes at the crowd level.** The packs draw the eyes at the full and medium levels only, but the crowd body (MakeHuman's 741-vertex proxy) has open eye sockets, so a person at the crowd level looked out of two pale holes. Crowd levels (`merge`) now draw the eyes too: one small draw more per person, no shadow (`smallPartShadows`). `ThreeActor.crowdEyes = false` turns it off.

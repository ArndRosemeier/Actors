## No see-through lace

- **Only the opaque parts of a garment hide the skin.** The importer used to hide the skin under every face of a MakeHuman garment, even where its texture is transparent (lace, cut-outs). The Long-sleeved gown (Medieval · Lady) has a see-through lace band above the bodice, so you looked through her chest at the background. Faces whose texture is mostly transparent no longer hide anything; regenerate the packs (`npm run import:human`).
- The medieval check (`apps/viewer/scripts/medieval.mjs`) takes a chest close-up of every female outfit and fails if the background shows through there.

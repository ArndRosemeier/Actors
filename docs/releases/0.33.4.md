## Bones bound in the actor's own frame: no stretched bodies when a host dresses a character off the scene

- **Stretched crowd bodies.** MasterWorld sometimes showed a townsperson as huge flat triangles streaming tens of metres from their hands. It dresses people with their place taken off the scene, so the place's world matrix still held the scene's offset (41 km at the start town). `rebind` brought the bones up to date from that stale parent (`updateMatrixWorld`). The eyes' bones then read the head's chain in world space, which recomputes that chain from the detached parent (`updateWorldMatrix`). The limbs were bound in one frame and the root, spine and head in the other, so every limb's inverse was 41 km off. The crowd mesh, which skins the limbs straight from those inverses, stretched until the next rebind. `rebind` now brings the actor's ancestors up to date first, so every bone is bound in the same frame. A test binds an actor detached from a scene 41 km out.

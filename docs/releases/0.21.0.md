## Deform: the giant recipe

`deform({ recipe: "giant", seed, strength })` in `@actors/core` makes a seeded plan that grows a body the way a giant is built, and the packages apply it.

- **Core:** `deform`, `DeformPlan`, `partOfBone` / `classifyBones` (which part a bone is, by name and by its parent), `deformBones`, `deformShapeOptions`, `mergeDeform`. Same seed and strength, same body; strength 0 changes nothing.
- **People:** `CharacterConfig.deform` (and `Human.deform`) lays a plan over the race: bone proportions and growth.
- **Generated animals:** `deformShape` and `deformCoat` in `@actors/animal` (the dog, deer and cow, kept inside each species' ranges).
- **Library animals:** `LibraryAnimal.setDeform(plan)`: part scales on the bones after every animation step, feet kept on the ground, a tint on a shared material clone (no new shader), clips slowed by the plan's gait.

Giants are not scaled copies: legs and neck get thicker than the size alone would give, the head and the shin lag, the jaw is heavy, the coat is old and grey at the muzzle, and the gait is slow. Recipes for the plague's twisted bodies follow and share the same plan. See docs/using-actors.md, "Deform: giants".

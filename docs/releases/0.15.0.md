## Hands that hold things

- `ThreeActor.setGrip(side, amount)`: closes the left or right hand (0 = the clip's own fingers, 1 = a fist) over whatever the clip does, so a held sword sits in a closed fist even in clips that keep the fingers open.
- `fistCurls(skeleton, side)` in `@actors/core`: the curl of each finger joint toward the palm, for renderers of their own.

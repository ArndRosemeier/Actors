## Officers' knees and the back of the neck are closed

Two more leftovers of the torn-coats fix.

- **Knees.** Officers (and midshipmen) wear the library's high socks under the navy breeches. The breeches ended above where the socks start, so skin showed in a wedge at the back of each knee at every detail level. The breeches now end lower (`legHole` at 0.64 of the leg instead of 0.6).
- **Back of the neck.** The tailcoat and the marine's coatee had a hole in the upper back, with the waistcoat or skin showing through (also at full detail). The coat's round neck opening is smaller (0.055 instead of 0.082), so the standing collar covers its edge.
- **Crowd level.** The shirt, jacket, breeches and gaiters keep more triangles at the distant levels (a coarse edge opened white gaps at the seaman's jacket collar); the neck stock is not reduced at all.

The edge of the stock at the throat is still a little ragged: that is the resolution of the cut, not a layering fault.

Needs a regenerated pack (`npm run import:human`); the release's packs have it. No API changes.

## Medieval wardrobe

Twelve "Medieval · …" outfit presets: peasant, peasant woman, traveller, archer, man-at-arms, mail knight, plate knight, noble, lady, merchant, nun and plague doctor.

- New procedural `med-*` garments: linen and long tunics, footless hose, leather jerkin, quilted gambeson, mail hauberk and coif, surcoat, tabard, breastplate, pauldrons, vambraces, greaves, belt and a short shoulder mantle. They are cut from the body and bend with the skin, and layers worn over each other move alike.
- 15 more MakeHuman pack pieces: tunics, a chemise, gowns, boots, shoes, a veil and a plague-doctor mask (14 CC-BY, credited in NOTICE-ASSETS.md, plus one CC0 hood).
- Fabric looks for weave, quilted, leather, mail and plate; mail and plate render as metal and look best with an environment map.
- The `knight` and `peasant` presets keep their ids with new contents.
- There are no long cloaks: without cloth simulation the arms show through them.

The garments live in the human asset pack, so regenerate or download the 0.10.0 packs. See [docs/medieval-wardrobe.md](https://github.com/ArndRosemeier/Actors/blob/v0.10.0/docs/medieval-wardrobe.md).

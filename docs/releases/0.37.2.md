## Navy garments stand clear of each other, so the crowd's coats stay whole

The second half of the torn-coats fix (0.37.1 fixed the drawing offsets of full characters under a reversed depth buffer). A crowd-level figure is one merged mesh with no drawing offsets, so its layers have only their geometry to go by, and the shirt, waistcoat and trousers poked through the coat at the shoulders and sleeves.

- The navy kit's garments now name the ones worn under them (`over` in `MedievalSpec`). At import each vertex is lifted along its normal until it stands 4 mm (trims 8 to 12 mm, where the crowd level's coarse coat sags under them) outside the surface of those (`nestMoves`, tools/import-makehuman/src/nest.ts). Garments are built inner first; the pack's order is unchanged.
- The neck stock keeps more triangles at the distant levels, so the skin no longer shows through its coarse chords at the collar.

Needs a regenerated pack (`npm run import:human`); the release's packs have it. No API changes.

## Ridable horses

A new animal: a horse you can ride (`animals.html?animal=horse` in the viewer).

- The body is a bay Thoroughbred-type gelding of 1.60 m at the withers, modelled on a reference photo, on the rig and 13 clips of Mesh2Motion's CC0 low-poly horse: idle, eat, sleep, walk, trot, gallop, turn left and right, rear, kick, headbutt, get up and death. About 64k triangles with short shell fur, a long mane and tail, and dark hooves.
- Nine presets: bay, chestnut, black, dapple grey, palomino, buckskin, Arabian, draught horse and pony. Sliders for size, legs, back, neck, head, ears, girth, bone, tail, crest, belly and dished face; coat colours with black points, dapples, face markings and socks.
- Tack: saddlecloth, saddle, stirrups, bridle and bit as optional meshes (tag `tack`), hidden until you show them.
- Riding: the pack has a `saddle` bone (`manifest.extra.animal.seat`). `Mount` in `@actors/three` keeps a human on the saddle through every gait, and `RIDE` in `@actors/human` is the seated pose. See "Riding (horses)" in docs/using-actors.md.

Regenerate or download the 0.11.0 animal packs to get the horse.

## Cows

- **A new animal: the cow** (`@actors/animal` species `cow`, pack `cow`). It is a Braunvieh-type cow built on the horse's skeleton, so it plays the horse's clips (walk, trot, gallop, eat, sleep and more; not `rear`). It has an udder with teats, cloven hooves with dewclaws, a dewlap, horn meshes, and an optional alpine collar with a bell (tag `bell`).
- **Eight breed presets:** braunvieh (the default), holstein, fleckvieh, hereford, angus, jersey, highland and charolais. Shape sliders include bulk, muscle, neck thickness, a dished face, udder size, horns (none to short) and long horns.
- **New coat options** for the fur material: `pied` and `piedSize` (patches), `udderSkin` and `darkHead`.
- **Fix:** the horse's eyes now follow the head. They were bound to a bone that doesn't move, so they floated in place during `eat` and `sleep`.
- **Fix:** white face markings at full strength no longer speckle the body with white.

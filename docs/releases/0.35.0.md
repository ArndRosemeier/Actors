## Golden age of Hollywood

- **A 1920s-50s wardrobe with fifteen presets** in a new "Golden age" group: Flapper, Screen siren, Showgirl, Starlet, Dame in furs, Secretary, Sock hop, Pin-up, Gala, Gangster, Detective, Leading man, Top hat and tails, Newsboy and City gent. Some are everyday, some deliberately over the top (a showgirl's fan of fifteen tall plumes, a feather boa, a diamond collar).
- **Generated pieces** (`ga-*`): trench coat, suspenders, long pearls, diamond collar, drop earrings, feather boa, fox fur stole, flapper headband and showgirl headdress. Necklaces and boas are hung on the body at import by a small chain simulation, so they rest on the collarbones and over the chest. Feathers and fur are white, alpha-cut cards, so any garment colour tints them.
- **21 MakeHuman pack garments** (CC-BY, credited in NOTICE-ASSETS.md): satin evening gown, little black dress, long halter gown, winter coat, polka-dot circle skirt, pencil skirt, retro top, shirt and tie, dress trousers, 1950s swimsuit, strapless bodysuit, saddle shoes, Mary Janes, oxfords, monk straps, bowler hat, top hat, trilby, flat cap and a handbag.
- Everything is tagged `vintage`, and period suits, the office outfit, fishnets, work boots and tinted glasses now fit the vintage theme too.
- New fabrics `fur`, `feather`, `pearl` and `gem` (`gem` is drawn as metal).
- New check: `apps/viewer/scripts/golden-age.mjs` shoots every golden age preset. See docs/golden-age-wardrobe.md. Regenerate the packs (`npm run import:human`).

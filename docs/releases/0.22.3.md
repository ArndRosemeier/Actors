## Library animals: `standing`, all feet on the ground of an animal that stands about

A clip's idle may dangle or lift a leg (the stylised tortoise's hind legs hung 0.3 to 1.5 m over the sand). `libraryAnimal.standing = true` makes the foot pass count every foot as planted and reach all of them down (more rounds, each pulling down), so a standing animal does not hover. Set it each frame from the animal's speed: true for idle, feeding, watching; false for walking and running, where a foot in the air is part of the gait. It does nothing without a ground set.

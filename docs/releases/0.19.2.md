## Horse tail and eyes

- **Tail:** the horse's and cow's tails no longer swing forward between the hind legs. The source rig's tail rests straight out behind, so every clip turned our hanging tail forward under the belly. Tail motion is now measured from the source's hanging idle tail (`tailNeutral` in the importer's clip options).
- **Horse eyes:** flatter and set into the head. The eyeball is flattened and sunk to the skin, and it shows through an almond-shaped opening between arched lids instead of bulging out as a round ball.

Regenerate or download the 0.19.2 animal packs to get the changes.

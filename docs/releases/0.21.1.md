## Deform: giants of any size

`deform({ recipe: "giant", seed, strength, size })` takes an optional `size` (1 to `MAX_GROWTH`, 24): grow the body this many times its normal size, instead of the 2.6 times that strength 1 gives. The proportions follow the size by its logarithm (a body 7 times as big has thicker legs, neck and jaw than one 2.6 times as big, but not 7/2.6 times as thick, and they stop growing at about 2.5 times the recipe), the gait slows by 1/sqrt(size), and the skin is still the strength's. No `size` gives exactly what 0.21.0 gave.

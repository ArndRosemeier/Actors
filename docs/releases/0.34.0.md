## A creator with Simple and Detailed tabs per section, and what a game allows

- **Simple and Detailed per section.** The editor's global Simple/Detailed switch is gone. Every section (race, robot, skeleton, body, face and hair, clothes, animation) has its own two tabs: Simple picks presets (gender, age, body type, height, ethnicity, hairstyle, outfit preset, robot palette, clip, expression), Detailed shows every control of that section. Both edit the same character, so Detailed opens on what Simple picked. Skin moved into Body, hair and the face shape into the new Face & hair section ("look" still works as its old name in `sections`), and the fine detail sliders into the Detailed tabs of Body and Face. The kind (person, robot, skeleton) is a picker in Race.
- **The 3026 clothes are an ordinary category.** The separate "3026 pants" picker is gone; the pieces are in the Clothes section's Detailed tab like every other garment.
- **`allow`.** `Character.load` and `createCharacterGenerator` take `allow: { races, clothing, height }`: races (with `robot` and `skeleton`) and clothing categories (`modern`, `vintage`, `future`, `antique`, `medieval`, `nordic`, `eastern`, `fantasy`) as a list or as flags, and a standing height range in meters. Bodies outside the range are scaled as a whole to the nearest limit. `ALLOW_PRESETS` has `city`, `vintage`, `fantasy` and `scifi`. The viewer takes `?allow=city` and `?races=…&clothing=…&minHeight=…&maxHeight=…`; `apps/viewer/scripts/allow.mjs` checks it.
- A catalog without `robot` or `skeletal` now also keeps `randomize` and `set` from making robots or skeletons (it already kept them out of the editor).

Hosts that styled the editor: the switch's `.ae-modes` is now `.ae-tabs` in each section header (`button[data-tab]`, `aria-selected`), and the face section's `data-section` is `face` (it was `look`).

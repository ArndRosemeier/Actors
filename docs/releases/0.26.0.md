## Expression wrinkles

Faces moved, but the skin stayed perfectly smooth: raised brows left no lines on the forehead, a smile no crow's feet. A new, optional `wrinkles` setting ("Wrinkles" in the editor) draws expression wrinkles in the skin shader:

- **Forehead lines** when the brows go up (surprise, fear), wavy and broken, dipping toward the temples.
- **Frown lines**: the vertical lines between the brows and the line across the nose root when the brows go down or the nose wrinkles (anger, disgust).
- **Crow's feet** fanning from the outer eye corners and the **nasolabial fold** from the nose wing past the mouth corner when the face smiles or squints.
- **Age**: from about 35 some of each stays at rest, deepest at 85 and up.

Their depth follows the face units every frame, so they work with the expression presets, the face rig and the living face. The importer draws the crease patterns once from face landmarks (eyes, teeth) into the free channels of the third skin mask (`skin_mask_c.png`: green forehead, blue frown, alpha smile); both skin shaders (WebGL and WebGPU) read the depth and its slope and shade the creases slightly. No wrinkle showing costs nothing; a showing one costs four extra texture reads per skin pixel. `ThreeActor.setWrinkles` and `wrinkleWeights` (`@actors/human`) drive them directly.

Re-run `npm run import:human` (or install the new release pack) for the wrinkle masks; older packs draw no wrinkles.

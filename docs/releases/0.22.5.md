## Library animals: a standing animal's legs may bend farther, and its rounds stop when they stop gaining

The giant turtle's right hind leg hit the 0.7 rad bend limit at rest and hovered; every frame also ran the full ten reach rounds (2.4 ms). While `standing`, each leg bone may now turn twice as far from the clip's pose, there are at most six rounds, and after three rounds they stop once a round gains less than a tenth on the best so far.

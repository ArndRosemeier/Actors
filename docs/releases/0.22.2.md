## Library animals: the guard only refuses a ground that is far under every foot

0.22.1 left an animal alone when any one sole was more than half the body's height in the ground. A cliff edge or a high step under a single foot did that, and the animal sank into it. The pass now refuses only when every sole is that far in (a wrong frame, a teleport); one foot that far in is lifted over like any other.

## A mouth that looks like one: modelled teeth, a tongue on the jaw, a dark mouth

Open-mouth expressions (surprise, laugh, kiss) showed the base mesh's teeth helper: a flat white, jagged plate, lit as brightly as the lips, with a tongue that stayed up while the jaw dropped.

- **Teeth and tongue** are now MakeHuman's CC0 proxies: modelled teeth and gums (3,900 vertices, with their texture) and the textured tongue, bound to the base mesh's helpers. Both get a baked shade (vertex colour) that darkens with depth behind the front teeth, so molars and the back of the tongue sit in the mouth's shadow.
- **The tongue rides on the jaw.** MakeHuman's "mouth open" dropped the lower teeth 3 cm and the tongue a few millimetres. The importer now adds the motion of the nearest lower teeth to every tongue vertex in every face unit.
- **The inside of the mouth is dark.** A third skin mask (`skin_mask_c.png`, red channel) marks the mouth cavity (skin with skin in front of it, around the teeth); both skin shaders darken it. The other three channels are free for later skin work.
- **Face proxies follow the face.** Teeth, tongue and the eyebrow meshes now move with expressions, blinking and the face rig (`followsFace` and `faceSpace` in `@actors/core`); before, eyebrow meshes stayed put when the brows went up. `surfaceDelta` returns their deltas under the mesh name.

Re-run `npm run import:human` (or install the new release pack): the teeth, tongue, targets and masks are in the pack. Older packs still work (no mouth mask: no darkening).

## Hair and beards as masses, not single hairs

Hair and beards are now drawn as solid, cohesive masses. Single strands no longer show.

**Beards.** A beard used to be 2,400 thin strand cards. It is now a solid shell over the beard area (the skin pushed out along its normal, so it follows the face in every expression) with a few hundred wide, layered locks on top. The shell ends inside a painted beard on the skin, so the edge thins out instead of ending in a line. Short beards and the chinstrap are painted only, with no geometry. The chinstrap is now a band along the jaw; before, it covered most of the cheeks.

- `beardPaint(style)` in `@actors/human` says how the skin under a beard is painted. `Character` applies it, so nothing changes for callers.
- The stubble uniform takes two more settings: `uStubble.x` above 1 fills the skin with the hair colour (a painted short beard), and `uStubble.w` blurs the outline so it fades out softly.
- The beard texture (`beardTexture`, which replaces `strandTexture` in `@actors/three`) has one lock with a soft root and pointed tips, and the shell's ragged edge.

**Hairstyles.** All 64 styles:

- The importer turns every hair texture into locks (`massHairPng`): gaps between strands close up, lone strands that stick out of a lock fade away, and fine strand contrast is softened. Brows keep their fine hairs.
- The runtime bends scalp-hair normals outward from the middle of the head (`hairShellNormals` in `@actors/core`), and the hair materials use that normal on both sides of a card. The cards light as one mass instead of each card by its own angle.
- Less sheen. The old strong, tight sheen lit up every card edge seen side-on as a bright strand (the white lines on the afro, for example).

Both renderers (WebGL and WebGPU) and all detail levels get this.

Re-run `npm run import:human` (or install the new release pack) for the new hair textures; the rest needs only a package update.

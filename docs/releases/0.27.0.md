## Clothes that stay on top

Inner layers kept poking through outer ones: socks through shoes, a shirt through a jacket, skin through trousers at the hip. A new, optional `clothTuck` setting ("Tuck covered layers in" in the outfit panel, on by default) tucks covered parts in under the garment over them:

- **Skin** under a covering garment sinks 3 mm under its surface.
- **Body-cut garments** (socks, tights, underwear, the generated T-shirt and jeans) drop down onto the skin where something covers them.
- **Fitted garments** (MakeHuman clothes) move most of the way toward the body points they hang from.
- **Same-layer garments** are now covered in outfit order: shoes cover socks, the later garment covers the earlier one.

Only the areas a garment already declares as covered are moved, so hems, cuffs and anything that hangs free stay where they were. It runs once per outfit or shape change on the CPU and costs nothing per frame. `ThreeActor.tuckCovered` switches it directly.

### A poke-through finder

`apps/viewer/scripts/clipping.mjs` (run against `npm run dev`) puts every outfit preset on a female and a male body, renders each mesh in its own flat colour from eight angles and reports patches of one mesh enclosed by another garment, grouped by pair (`OUTFITS=` picks presets, `DUMP_LABELS=1` writes marked images). Over the 14 presets on both bodies it found 3,996 poke-through patches before this release and 1,656 with tucking. Most of what remains is trousers against boots and tunics against trousers, where the outfit order or a garment's covered area needs fixing in the data.

## Tinted clothes keep their tint in sunlight

0.15.1 did not stop the skirts from going pale. MasterWorld's gored skirt still came out nearly white.

- **The importer averages garment textures in linear light.** A tint divides each texel's brightness by the texture's mean brightness. That mean was averaged over sRGB values, which reads too dark for a high-contrast texture, so every texel was pushed brighter. The mean is now averaged in linear light and still stored as sRGB. The packs are regenerated.
- **The soft knee tops out at 1.5 instead of 2.** Bright patches stay a little lighter than the tint instead of approaching white. Hair uses the same knee.
